# Club fact check, phase 1a: venue, capacity, tier, coordinates, name (2026-11)

A seeded sample audit of the map's club layer (`data/clubs/<CC>.json`, 600 clubs on 2026-11-11: 217 hand-corrected through `data/clubs-manual.csv`, 383 from Wikidata alone). This section (method, seed, sample, leads, verdict rules) was written and committed **before any page was read**. Results follow further down.

## Method

- **Frame**: every club in `data/clubs/??.json` at commit `4d70fe3` (600). Two halves: **lower** = tier 3 and lower (148: DE 113, RO 35), **upper** = tiers 1 and 2 (452, 13 countries).
- **Seed**: `20261111`, Python `random.Random(20261111)`, one generator used in this order: lower half then upper half; inside a half the countries in alphabetical order; inside a country `hand` before `wd`; `rng.sample()` over the club ids sorted as strings.
- **Allocation**: 30 clubs per half. Countries get seats in proportion to their clubs in that half (largest remainder, at least one seat per country with clubs there). Inside a country the seats are split between hand-corrected (`manual: true`) and Wikidata-only clubs in proportion (largest remainder, no minimum).
- **Result of the allocation**: lower DE 23, RO 7; upper AT 2, BE 2, CH 1, DE 2, ES 3, FR 2, GB 3, GR 2, IT 3, NL 3, PT 2, RO 3, RS 2. Hand-corrected 21 of 60 (frame: 217 of 600), Wikidata-only 39.
- **The five German clubs of tiers 6 to 8 were left in the frame** (the job draws "from the map's club files"). One was drawn (FC Kray). It is judged like any other sample club, but none of the five is changed whatever the verdict: that is Alexandru's decision.
- A club that is both in the sample and on the leads list (Hamburger SV, 1. FC Kaiserslautern II, FC Kray) counts in the statistics once, as a sample club.

## Verdict rules (fixed before reading)

Fields: home ground 2026/27, capacity, league and tier 2026/27, coordinates, club name. Verdicts: `correct`, `wrong`, `stale`, `disputed`, `cannot verify`.

- Sources that may judge: the club's, league's or federation's own site; the text of a Wikipedia article (all languages are ONE source); StadiumDB; OpenStreetMap (position of the ground, and its `capacity` tag as a figure). **Wikidata judges nothing**: it is read only to learn which item and which articles the club is, and what the map was built from.
- **Capacity**: `correct` when the map's figure is within 3 percent of a current figure an independent source states. `wrong` only when no source is within 3 percent AND the evidence is solid: two independent sources within 3 percent of each other, or one source that is the club, league or federation, or a Wikipedia figure that carries a cited reference. `disputed` when the sources disagree with each other by more than 3 percent and the map sits inside or outside the range without a solid majority (the range is recorded, nothing is corrected), or when the differing figure belongs to another configuration (league against international, seated against total). `cannot verify` when no independent figure was found, or when the only differing figure is a single uncited one (the figure is recorded and counted separately, not as an error).
- **Home ground**: `correct` when a source names the same ground for 2026/27 (a sponsor name against the traditional name of the same ground is the same ground; the source's wording is recorded). `stale` when the club played there and has moved. `wrong` when the source names another ground and nothing says the club ever used the map's one.
- **Tier**: `correct` when the 2026/27 season article of the league the map gives (the article named in `data/league-rosters.csv`) lists the club. `wrong` or `stale` when another division's 2026/27 article lists it. `cannot verify` when no 2026/27 season article lists it.
- **Coordinates**: `correct` when the map's point is within 500 m of the OpenStreetMap position of the ground the sources name (Nominatim search by the ground's name, or a named pitch or stadium found by Overpass around the map's point). `wrong` when OpenStreetMap has that ground more than 500 m away. `cannot verify` when OpenStreetMap has no object that can be tied to the ground by name.
- **Name**: `correct` when it is the club's name as the Wikipedia article title or infobox gives it, in any language (legal suffixes and "F.C." spellings aside). `wrong` when it names another club or a former name.
- Own knowledge produces leads only, marked "from memory, unverified".

# Results (2026-11-11)

## How it was read

- Draft PR #210, temporary workflow `probe-audit.yml`, **3 runs used of the 8 allowed** (a first push of run 1 had a syntax error of the session's own making and stopped before any request; it does not count). Each run posted what it read as comments on PR #210: one comment per club with the Wikidata item, the infobox, the lead text, the capacity paragraphs and the external links of each article; one comment per season article with its table rows; the machine-readable summary; the request log.
- Run 1 (325 requests): the Wikidata item of each of the 66 clubs and of its grounds; the club's article in en.wikipedia and in the country's language, and the ground article each infobox links; the 2026/27 season articles of `data/league-rosters.csv` for the 20 leagues in the sample (and their local-language versions); StadiumDB's 13 country tables (`robots.txt` first: it disallows only `/lay-gfx/`); Nominatim; Overpass.
- Run 2 (95 requests): the 2026-27 Regionalliga and Liga III articles again with every table (run 1 had skipped the league tables), the five de.wikipedia Regionalliga division articles named in the link list of `Fußball-Regionalliga 2026/27`, Nominatim and Overpass again.
- Run 3 (16 requests): five de.wikipedia Oberliga 2026/27 articles from the same link list, and text passages of ten club articles.
- **Sources that judged**: Wikipedia (one source, whatever the language), StadiumDB, OpenStreetMap. **No club, league or federation site was opened** (each needs its own robots and terms check; there was no room for 66). So "two independent sources" here always means Wikipedia plus StadiumDB or OpenStreetMap.
- **Hosts that stopped the probe**: Nominatim answered HTTP 503 after 9 clubs in run 1 and was not asked again in that run; in run 2 it was asked again at one request per 2.6 seconds, answered 503 twice, and each time answered after one wait. Overpass answered 502 for two of three batches in run 1 and worked in run 2 after one retry. No host refused for good. Nothing was routed around.
- **Limits of the reading**: capacity figures are the first number of an infobox line (an infobox that lists historic or concert capacities can add odd figures to the "all figures" lists below); a ground is tied to OpenStreetMap by name; the tier check rests on Wikipedia's season articles alone.
