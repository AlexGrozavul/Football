# Bucket list audit, phase 3b (November 2026)

Fact check of the Bucket list (`bucketList` and two `ticketEvents` of
`data/football-rules.json`): what exists (Part A), a mapping of the derby
entries to `data/derbies.csv` (Part B, report only), a seeded sample of 60
"why" texts read against pages (Part C) and the two past ticket events
(Part D). This file was written in two steps: Parts A and B and the method
and sample of Part C first, **before any page was read** (its own commit),
the results after.

## Part A: what exists

- **Where**: `bucketList` in `data/football-rules.json`, 215 entries. Every
  entry has `id`, `type` (`fixture` 172, `event` 36, `experience` 7), `title`,
  `group`, `country` (a name, or `none`), `countryCode`, `sortKey` and `kind`
  (`derby` 167, `final` 27, `tournament` 7, `playoff` 6, `national-team` 4,
  `event` 2, `cup-round` 1, `other` 1). 199 carry `listOnly: true` (kept off
  the calendar), 102 `leagueCheck: true`. The 18 older hand-written entries
  also carry `priority` and `status` (`open` 13, `deferred` 3, `blocked` 2)
  and, some of them, `venue`, `clubs`, `trigger`, `ranks`, `distanceKm`,
  `nextFixture`, `fixtures`, `ticketNote` and the like.
- **The "why" text** is the plain string `why` (211 of 215 entries; the four
  without one are the four play-off entries added 2026-10-08,
  `zweite-liga-relegation`, `regionalliga-aufstiegsspiele`,
  `superliga-relegation-baraj` and `liga3-promotion-tournament`, which carry
  a `whyAssistant` note instead; `pokal-first-round` and `liga2-playoff`
  have a `why` but no `whyStatus`). 20 entries carry a second text, `whyAssistant` ("added note").
  This audit checks `why` only.
- **`whyStatus`** is a string on 213 entries and its only value anywhere is
  `unverified` (213 times). No other value is used in the data, read by the
  page, named in the docs or accepted by a test: conventions.md says "a
  verification pass flips `whyStatus` entry by entry" but never names the
  value it flips to, the page only asks `whyStatus === 'unverified'`, and
  `tools/test_calendar.js` requires `unverified` on the 48 entries of
  2026-10-10 and on the play-off entries. **So the field has no value that
  means checked today.**
- **Sources**: `whySources` (a list of URLs, drawn as "Sources for the note")
  is on 5 entries only, all play-off entries (`relegation`,
  `zweite-liga-relegation`, `regionalliga-aufstiegsspiele`,
  `superliga-relegation-baraj`, `liga3-promotion-tournament`); they are the
  sources of the `whyAssistant` note, and none was opened when it was written.
  The other 206 "why" texts have no source. `test_calendar.js` forbids
  `whySources` on the 48 entries of 2026-10-10 (its `NOT_ALLOWED` list).
- **`ticketEvents`**: 11 entries, each with `id`, `club`, `title`, `action`,
  `leadTimeDays`, `feed` and `dateSource`; the date is `date` (2 entries),
  `dateEstimate` (8) or `nextEstimate` (2). There is **no `active` field**.
  A `status` field exists on exactly one entry (`fcb-away-window-2026`,
  `status: "missed"`); the page does not read it, `build_calendars.py` only
  prints it. "missed" says he did not act in a window, not that a date has
  passed, so **no value that means past exists**.
- **Does the app show `whyStatus`?** Yes: an entry whose `whyStatus` is
  `unverified` gets the tag "Not yet verified" on its Bucket list card, and
  its added note is labelled "not verified" (`index.html`, two places).

## Part B: derby entries against `data/derbies.csv` (report only)

Method. For each of the 167 entries with `kind` `derby`: (1) **by ids** -
the `derbies.csv` row whose `rulesId` is the entry's id, or whose two Q-ids
are exactly the entry's two Q-ids (from `bucket-links-manual.csv` and, for
the older entries, `clubs` through `football-rules-links.csv`); (2) **by
name only** - the two club names of the title ("A v B", brackets and a
leading rivalry name set aside) against `clubAName` and `clubBName`: accents
and case folded, legal-form words (FC, SV, 1., City, United and the like)
dropped, the entry's words equal to or a subset of the row's, a reserve side
only with a reserve side, "lyonnais" read as "Lyon"; failing that, the row's
derby name found as whole words in the title while at least one club name
shares a word; (3) otherwise no row. Nothing was changed in either file.

## Part C: the "why" texts - method and sample (written before any page was read)

- **Population**: the 211 `bucketList` entries with a non-empty `why`.
- **Seed**: 20261116. **Sample size**: 60.
- **Draw** (systematic sampling, which spreads the sample over kinds and
  countries in proportion): the 211 entries are sorted by `id`, shuffled
  with Python `random.Random(20261116).shuffle`, then stably sorted by
  (`kind`, `country`), so the shuffled order survives inside each kind and
  country cell. Step = 211 / 60 = 3.5167; start = `rng.random() * step` from
  the same generator (2.2882); the entries at positions
  `int(start + i * step)`, i = 0 ... 59, are the sample.
- **Result by kind** (population -> sample): derby 167 -> 48, final 27 -> 8,
  tournament 7 -> 2, national-team 4 -> 1, playoff 2 -> 1, event 2 -> 0,
  cup-round 1 -> 0, other 1 -> 0.
- **Result by country**: Germany 22, England 5, no country 5, France 2,
  Italy 2, Poland 2, Romania 2, Scotland 2, Spain 2, Switzerland 2, and one
  each for Argentina, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Chile, Croatia, Egypt,
  Hungary, Mexico, Netherlands, Portugal, Slovenia, United States, Wales.
- **What is checked**: only the factual claims of `why` (dates, years,
  records, numbers, names, "oldest", "largest", firsts, who plays where,
  which league). Opinions, reputations ("loudest", "biggest fixture",
  "most intense") and recommendations are not checked.
- **Verdicts**: correct, wrong, unsupported, no factual claim, cannot verify.
- **Pass rule**: 0 or 1 entries wrong or unsupported passes; 2 or more means
  a full check of the remaining 151 texts later.
